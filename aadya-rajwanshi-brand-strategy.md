## SECTION 1 — BRAND ANALYSIS

### Current Website Audit

The current site at aadyarajwanshi.com is built on WordPress + Elementor — a generic drag-and-drop template with no custom identity, no cinematic feel, and no emotional storytelling. Here is a detailed breakdown of what is weak, outdated, and missing.

**What Is Weak**

- **The logo** is a stock "Black & White Simple Classic Acoustic Guitar" Canva logo — literally named in the file path. This destroys any premium perception instantly.
- **The hero tagline** — *"A Young Voice Bridging Cultures Through Music"* — is vague, generic, and reads like a school speech opener. It conveys nothing emotionally powerful.
- **The stat counters** display zeroes (0k followers, 0+ awards, 0+ shows) — this is worse than having no stats at all. It signals inactivity and inauthenticity.
- **The events section** lists a June 2024 Holi Festival in *Indonesia* and a July 2020 Diwali event — also in Indonesia. These are either incorrect, fictional, or outdated by years. This critically damages credibility.
- **The testimonials** read as clearly AI-generated ("Aadya Rajwanshi's music speaks to the heart and soul" — duplicated twice under different names). Fake testimonials are a severe trust destroyer.
- **"Book Singing Lessons on SuperProf"** — This is a catastrophic brand positioning error. Linking a music teaching platform in the footer signals that Aadya is a *tutor*, not a *performer*. This must be removed entirely from the main artist website.

**What Is Outdated**

- The entire layout is a 2019-era WordPress music template with boxed sections, hard horizontal rules, and no scroll-based animation.
- Typography is default system fonts — no character, no elegance.
- The color scheme (black and white) could work cinematically but is implemented flatly — no depth, no gradient, no texture.
- There is no mobile-first thinking. The layout collapses awkwardly on phones.
- Two separate YouTube channels (English and Hindi) create user confusion and dilute brand focus.

**What Is Missing**

- No above-the-fold cinematic hero image or video. The first impression is just text and a logo.
- No prominent **The Voice Kids UK** call-out. This is a major mainstream TV credential and is buried.
- No **BRIT School Croydon** badge or section. This is one of the most prestigious music schools in the UK (alumni: Adele, Amy Winehouse, FKA Twigs) and it is completely absent.
- No Spotify embed or streaming preview — the music section links out rather than playing in-page.
- No press/media coverage section.
- No professional EPK (Electronic Press Kit) for industry bookers.
- No real booking call-to-action with urgency.
- No email list capture with incentive.
- No Instagram feed integration.
- No performance reel or showreel video.

**How Branding Can Become Premium**

1. Replace the guitar logo with an elegant wordmark — just "AADYA" in a refined serif or editorial sans-serif, optionally with a small icon mark.
2. Remove every amateur signal (SuperProf link, Canva graphics, zero counters, fake testimonials).
3. Lead with the strongest credentials: ITV's The Voice Kids UK + BRIT School Croydon.
4. Build an emotional narrative: *From a 10-year-old who stunned ITV judges to a BRIT School-trained songwriter releasing originals at 16.*
5. Use cinematic photography: dark, moody studio shots, warm stage lighting, close-up vocal performance frames.
6. Let the music play inside the website — embedded Spotify and YouTube players feel immersive.

**Emotional Positioning**

Aadya should be positioned as *the next generation of British-South Asian artistry* — someone who carries the depth of Indian classical and Bollywood music and fuses it with the vulnerability and production sensibility of modern English pop. Think: Raye meets AR Rahman. Anoushka Shankar meets Olivia Rodrigo in sonic DNA. The emotional hook is *authenticity across cultures* — not as a gimmick, but as a genuine identity.

## SECTION 2 — WEBSITE STRATEGY

### Brand Direction

**Core Identity Statement:** *Aadya Rajwanshi is a BRIT School-trained, ITV-featured British-South Asian singer-songwriter whose music lives in the space where Bollywood soul meets contemporary pop vulnerability.*

**Emotional Tone:** Intimate. Elevated. Quietly powerful. Not loud — *felt*.

**Visual Identity Direction:**

- **Era:** Cinematic Dark Luxury. Think: A24 film poster + Apple Music artist page + Vogue editorial.
- **Mood board keywords:** Candlelight, velvet, obsidian, gold leaf, fog, stage smoke, microphone close-ups, raw emotion.
- **NOT:** Colourful Canva graphics, stock photo smiles, confetti, generic gradient backgrounds.

### Typography System

| Role | Font | Style |
|------|------|-------|
| Display / Hero | Cormorant Garamond | Italic Light — cinematic, editorial |
| Headings | Playfair Display | Semi-Bold — elegant authority |
| Body | DM Sans | Regular — clean, modern, readable |
| Accent / Labels | Bebas Neue or Letter Gothic | Uppercase tracking — music industry feel |
| Quote Callouts | Cormorant Garamond | Large Italic — emotional pull |

### Color Palette

```
Primary Background:   #0A0A0A  (near-black — cinematic depth)
Secondary Surface:    #111111  (rich dark — card backgrounds)
Text Primary:         #F5F0E8  (warm cream — not harsh white)
Text Secondary:       #9A9080  (warm grey — subtitles, captions)
Accent Gold:          #C9A96E  (muted gold — luxury premium touch)
Accent Warm Amber:    #E8B86D  (hover states, highlights)
Soft Border:          #222222  (subtle dividers)
Gradient Overlay:     rgba(10,10,10,0.75) on images
```

### Scrolling Experience

- **Parallax hero:** Background image scrolls at 0.6x speed, creating cinematic depth.
- **Fade-in reveals:** Every section fades in and slides up 24px as it enters viewport (IntersectionObserver).
- **Horizontal scroll:** Featured tracks section scrolls horizontally on desktop.
- **Sticky navigation:** Navigation becomes a transparent-to-frosted-glass header on scroll.
- **Full-screen section transitions:** Each major section occupies 100vh with smooth snap-scroll feel.
- **Cursor custom:** A subtle dot cursor on desktop with a slow trailing circle — premium feel.

### Premium Interaction Ideas

1. **Magnetic buttons:** Booking/CTA buttons respond to cursor proximity (magnetic hover effect).
2. **Music player:** Custom minimal floating audio player that persists while scrolling.
3. **Video hover reveal:** Thumbnail images reveal embedded YouTube on hover with a smooth fade.
4. **Text mask reveal:** Hero headline text appears masked behind a moving gradient on load.
5. **Noise texture overlay:** Subtle film grain CSS overlay across the whole site (opacity: 3-5%) for analogue warmth.
6. **Logo morph:** Artist name subtly scales to 1.02x on hover with letter-spacing expansion.

## SECTION 7 — VISUAL DIRECTION

### Photography Style Guide

**Hero / Feature Images:**
- Shot in a professional studio or atmospheric indoor location (backstage, dark corridor, ambient stage lighting)
- Lighting: Single dramatic key light from the side — chiaroscuro feel. Warm amber/candlelight tones.
- Pose: Thoughtful. Looking away from camera, or a direct gaze with vulnerability. Not smiling — feeling.
- Colour grade: Rich blacks, lifted shadows, warm highlights. Think: Lorde's "Solar Power" era, but darker.
- NOT: Bright outdoor photos, school-portrait smiles, or smartphone selfies.

**Performance Images:**
- Stage photography with natural performance lighting — spot lights, smoke, warm amber
- Motion blur acceptable (shows energy and presence)
- Vertical crops for mobile/Instagram; wide landscape crops for desktop heroes

**Music / Album Art:**
- Minimalist. Name + artwork. No clutter.
- Consistent colour language: dark background, warm/gold tones.
- Typographic consistency with website fonts.

**Gallery / Behind The Scenes:**
- Honest and real — in the studio, with headphones, writing lyrics
- Black and white for timeless elegance
- Can be slightly imperfect / editorial — authenticity matters

### Background Textures

- Subtle noise/film grain overlay (CSS: SVG filter or PNG texture at 3% opacity)
- Dark gradient from image edge inward (for text legibility)
- Velvet-like dark purples and blacks in section backgrounds
- Occasionally: a narrow gold gradient line as a section divider
