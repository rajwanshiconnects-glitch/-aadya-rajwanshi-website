The Artist

"At 10, I stood on ITV and sang in two languages. At 16, I'm writing the songs that only I can write."

— Aadya Rajwanshi

Aadya Rajwanshi is a British-South Asian singer-songwriter training at the BRIT School in Croydon — the institution that shaped Adele, Amy Winehouse, and FKA Twigs.

She first came to national attention in 2020, walking onto the ITV stage for The Voice Kids UK at just ten years old. Her bilingual blind audition — fusing Sia's Cheap Thrills with the Bollywood classic Pehli Nazar Mein — left coaches speechless. She joined Team Pixie.

Since then, Aadya has been building something more personal: a body of original work that refuses to be defined by genre, language, or expectation.

Creative Initiative

Superclef

Born from Aadya's own journey as a young artist navigating the music industry, Superclef is a creative initiative dedicated to championing and supporting young musicians — amplifying voices that deserve to be heard.

Rooted in cultural pride and artistic mentorship, Superclef is where emerging talent finds community, opportunity, and a stage.
